# Ren NPR study v1: shader and material contract

Shader: `LucidLoop/Characters/PaintedAnimeNPR`, implemented in `Unity/Assets/CharacterArt/NPR/PaintedAnimeNPR.shader` and its adjacent HLSL. `ren-npr-presets.json` maps all 14 frozen V2 material names. This study changes shading only. The newly selected Studio H A-open face shape is a separate source; no geometry or UV acceptance follows from these comparisons.

**Color and material input.** `_BaseColor` is a linear RGBA **vector**, copied from the old material's `_LinearBaseColor`; `_UseBaseMap` controls the unchanged sRGB base texture. `_UseVertexColor` replaces the fallback color (matching the authored iris), and `_VertexColorSrgb` optionally decodes sRGB vertex data. Preset `colors[].linearRgba` values are already linear: use `SetVector` or `SetColor` without a second conversion. `_Unlit=1` returns exact source color, with no scene lighting. It is a diagnostic, not the NPR lighting implementation.

**Painted tones.** Runtime illumination selects deep shade, shade and base paint using separate thresholds and soft transition widths. `_ShadeColor` and `_DeepShadeColor` multiply the original paint; proposed warm/cool pigment ratios preserve brows, liner and iris detail. They are adjustable art-direction values, not artist-approved paint. Optional `_PAINT_REGIONS` adds one linear RGB map: R biases the base-band input, G biases the deep-band input, B permits broad highlights. It shares the base UVs. Current presets disable this map; no head-specific mask or invented painted-plane map is claimed.

**Lights and volume.** Main and additional URP lights use their actual color, inverse-distance/spot attenuation and shadow attenuation. Contributions are capped before soft tone selection; normalized weighted light hues tint the pigments with restrained strength. `_AmbientFloor` is a tone-control floor, not flat emitted RGB. There is no emission term in this shader. Broad highlight and rim are optional pigment blends, not a microfacet BRDF. Skin `_HighlightStrength=0` exactly; no normal map, reflection probe, skin specular lobe, clearcoat or outline pass is used.

`_FaceMode` blends mesh normals toward a broad analytic face-plane field using `_FaceForwardWS`, `_FaceRightWS`, `_FaceUpWS`, `_FaceCenterWS`, `_FaceWidth` (half width) and `_FaceCurvature`. The frame is UV-independent and must follow the animated head. Initial mode 1 flattened ears/neck and was rejected. Revised skin mode **0.45** retains most geometric volume. The viewer uses a root-local width reference rather than a rotating world AABB. Update cloned per-instance materials with `SetVector`; **do not use MaterialPropertyBlocks**, which break SRP Batcher use. All material properties share one `UnityPerMaterial` buffer across passes. This is the intended SRP Batcher layout, but the isolated headless compatibility query remained `Not initialized ()` after synchronous compilation and SetPass; runtime compatibility is therefore **unverified**, not certified.

**Depth, shadows and budget.** Opaque ZWrite, depth-only, geometric depth-normal and directional/punctual ShadowCaster passes are supplied. Main cascades/screen shadows, soft shadow tiers, additional-light shadows and rendering layers use installed URP **17.3.0** APIs from `RealtimeLights.hlsl`, `Lighting.hlsl` and `Shadows.hlsl`. The Forward+ loop uses the installed `USE_CLUSTER_LIGHT_LOOP` pattern. Per-pixel additional lights are the intended mode; vertex lights are an approximate compatibility fallback and do not provide the stable per-pixel face/shadow response. Current additional-light budget is **first 4**, at most 8. This can drop/change selected lights as cluster ordering changes. It is a preview constraint, not a production nightclub light-priority solution or a measured iPhone 15 Plus performance result. No motion-vector pass, lightmap Meta pass or deferred G-buffer path is supplied; this uses UniversalForwardOnly.

**Known paint limit.** The unchanged 2K V2 face atlas contains painted nose/form shading and bright lip marks. Removing added skin spec does not remove those baked marks. Uniformly flattening/emitting that image would hide lighting problems, not fix its albedo. A later artist-directed highlight/shadow cleanup or paint-mask revision is required on the selected final head. No new texture derivative was made while that source shape is changing.

**Evidence and acceptance.** The viewer agent owns isolated Unity compilation and actual matched front/quarter/club captures. First compile/player build had no reported errors; first old/NPR unlit PNGs were byte-identical. First neutral NPR was too flat and triggered the one documented six-property skin revision. Final used-variant/shadow/capture evidence belongs to the viewer report. Forward+ and additional-shadow source paths are not runtime-validated unless that report explicitly tests them. Do not call this completed Arcane/NPR style, full Ren likeness, or sustained 30fps device validation merely because it compiles.

Final bounded Unity proof: the study-owned pipeline enables Medium-filtered soft main shadows at 2048px, distance 6, one cascade. Revised neutral front/quarter and two moving-color phases were visually inspected; shadow aliasing is substantially reduced and broad shade regions preserve more volume than the initial flat preset. The viewer reports zero shader messages, all four passes present, 28 actual pose/light captures, and byte-identical old/NPR unlit images. See `unity-review/` for promoted build/live/capture evidence. Additional-light shadows remain disabled in this test, Forward+ remains untested, and no Metal/iOS build or 30fps phone measurement was performed. The visible baked lip marks and proposed rosy shade palette still require artist review on the selected final shape.
