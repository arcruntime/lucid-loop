## Fidelity observations

The reviewed images retain long half-lidded grey-blue eyes, tapered dark liner and lashes, slim resting brows, a compact nose-to-mouth interval, volumetric graphic rose lips and a tapered chin. Scalp hair and jewelry are removed; eyebrows and eyelashes remain. The full smooth scalp, ears and short neck are visible, with no shoulders or other accessories.

The initial [closed-rest draft](head-closed-rest-three-view.png) retained translucent fringe-shaped lines at the inner brows, nose bridge and temples. A targeted image-generation edit removed those marks in v2 while preserving eyelid/brow/lash details, skin shading and head contours. The parent accepted the skull proportions as plausible for this construction study.

The open-A pair uses a modest visible dark oral opening and restrained upper teeth. It is intended to give reconstruction an oral aperture without an extreme jaw drop. Generated cavity depth, lip-tooth separation and consistency between front and profile must be inspected on the resulting mesh. This is neither a completed viseme nor a claim of final Ren likeness approval. Scalp shape and unseen anatomy are inferred from the artist's design.
