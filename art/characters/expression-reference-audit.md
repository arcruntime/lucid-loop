# Character Expression Reference Audit

## Scope and evidence rules

- Production aesthetic: **Arcane/Fortiche-inspired** character rendering is the current direction and supersedes TOKON.
- This audit records only what is visibly drawn or printed on the five supplied reference images. Printed expression captions are transcribed as **artist labels**. Descriptions of facial action are **observations**. Any emotional reading beyond those captions is isolated as **inference**.
- For clothing, the repeated front/side/back turnaround is treated as the default design. A separately captioned casual or working view is treated as an alternate or supporting view according to its printed label.

## Player avatar

**Source:** `art/characters/player-avatar.png`

### Artist-labeled expressions (verbatim)

- **None.** The image is a four-angle clothing/body turnaround labeled only `FRONT`, `THREE-QUARTER`, `SIDE`, and `BACK`.

### Exact visible expression evidence

- Eyes are open with relaxed lids and brows in the front and three-quarter views.
- Gaze is generally forward in the front view and follows the head direction in three-quarter/side views.
- A fitted black mask fully covers the nose, mouth, cheeks, and chin, so no mouth shape or lower-face emotion is visible.

### Inferred readings

- The exposed eye/brow area reads neutral or attentive, but **no emotional label is authoritative**.
- The mask prevents reliable separation of neutral, concerned, guarded, or faintly smiling states. Any such label would need a new approved expression reference.

### Default clothing and identity anchors

- **Default and only shown look:** black fitted face mask; cropped black bomber jacket with a muted blue-grey pointed collar; plain black crew-neck top; high-waisted wide-leg black trousers; black low-heeled ankle boots.
- Long black hair in a high ponytail with loose waves, side-swept bangs, and two face-framing tendrils; dark brows; warm brown/amber eyes; small hoop earring visible in profile.
- Keep the high wavy ponytail, side fringe, black mask, blue-grey collar, cropped jacket, and wide-leg trouser silhouette recognizable at gameplay scale.

### Ambiguity

- The sheet provides no character name, age, pronouns, role, personality, expression set, accessories beyond the small visible earring, or alternate clothing.
- Hair volume and wave pattern vary slightly between angles; the consistent anchor is a long, high black ponytail with a soft wave and loose front strands.
- Because the face covering removes most facial landmarks, eye shape, brow shape, hair silhouette, collar color, and clothing proportions carry the avatar's identity.
