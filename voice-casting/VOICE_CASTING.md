First audition selections based on the four character sheets. These are creative casting proposals, not audio-tested matches. Regional influences come from the official voice documentation; the sheets do not specify accents. Delivery notes describe the intended performance rather than verified voice qualities.

## Luca → `meridian`

**40 · Bartender / information hub**  
**Voice profile:** Masculine; North American influence.

Calm, perceptive, relaxed, and difficult to read. His black shirt, burgundy apron, and subtle expressions suggest an understated presence.

**Direction:** Warm and measured, with comfortable pauses and occasional dry humor. He distinguishes what he heard from what he knows.

> He was here. What he meant by that… you’d have to ask him.

## Maya → `gleam`

**26 · Close friend / freelance photographer**  
**Voice profile:** Feminine; North American influence.

Warm, playful, loyal, and impulsive. Pink hair, expressive reactions, and bold streetwear support an outgoing performance.

**Direction:** Lively and spontaneous, with affectionate teasing. Quicker when excited; softer and sincere when concerned.

> One photo, then we go. …Hey. You’ve been quiet tonight. What’s up?

## Ren → `quartz`

**29 · Resident DJ / observer**  
**Voice profile:** Feminine; Australian influence.

Cool, magnetic, independent, and slightly aloof. Black clothing, silver details, and restrained expressions support a composed presence.

**Direction:** Relaxed, concise, and dryly amused. Comfortable with silence. Precise about what she saw versus what she couldn’t hear.

> I saw them leave together. Didn’t hear what they said. There’s a difference.

## Theo → `vesper`

**30 · Socialite**  
**Voice profile:** Masculine; British influence.

Charming and well connected. Emerald tailoring, gold accessories, and expansive gestures suggest confidence and theatrical flair.

**Direction:** Polished and animated, with playful emphasis and easy laughter. Quieter and more deliberate when sharing something sensitive.

> There you are. Come with me—there’s someone you should meet.

## Ensemble direction

- **Luca** gives the player space.
- **Maya** closes the distance.
- **Ren** makes the player work for her attention.
- **Theo** draws the player into his circle.
