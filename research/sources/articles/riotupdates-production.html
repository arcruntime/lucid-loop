How Arcane Was Made

Riot supplied the world, writing, financing, music, and production leadership. Fortiche turned it into performance: modeled 3D assets, deliberate keyframes, painterly surfaces, graphic 2D effects, light, color, and compositing.

Independent publication — no production access or affiliation is claimed.

By Riot Updates Research Desk Verified August 26, 2026 Sources 90

Was Arcane 2D or 3D animation?

Its core characters and environments were built and animated in 3D, then shaped with painted textures, stylized lighting, graphic 2D effects, matte work, and dense compositing. Fortiche described main-character performance as keyframed rather than motion-captured. Season Two's crowd team separately used simulation and some retargeted capture for background cycles; that does not make the lead performances motion capture.

A reset, not a straight six-year render Early work included tests, pitches, writing, and a substantial quality crisis. Riot announced a 2020 target in 2019, then delayed the series to 2021 as COVID-19 disrupted an international collaborative pipeline. The six-year shorthand describes the development journey, not six uninterrupted years of final-shot animation.
Sources: ARC-S001 ARC-S002 ARC-S068 ARC-S069
Budget figures need labels The widely repeated $250 million number was reported for both seasons plus marketing. Riot co-founder Marc Merrill later described the creation budget as a ballpark 60–75% of that total. Contracts and final show accounts are private, so neither figure should be presented as an audited animation budget.
