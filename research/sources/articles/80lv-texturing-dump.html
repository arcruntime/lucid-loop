A Closer Look at Texturing in Arcane

#News

Fortiche's amazing artists finally shared a behind-the-scenes look at some of the models prepared for the Nextflix show. 

You know we love art dumps and you might have noticed we're big fans of the Fortiche's style in the recent Nextflix hit called Arcane. Artists from the production team, and texturing artists in particular finally had a chance to share some of the things they contributed to and provide a look at the show's amazing textures.
