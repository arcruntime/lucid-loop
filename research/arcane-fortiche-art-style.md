# Arcane (Riot / Fortiche) art style: reimplementation survey

**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Question:** Has anyone published a Blender / Unity / Unreal / academic reimplementation of *Arcane*'s painterly NPR-adjacent look (Fortiche Production, Netflix / Riot Games), suitable as a base for recreating it in Unity?
**Verdict:** Yes — this look has been studied hard, mostly in **Blender** (offline, paint + compositing) and **Unreal** (real-time environments). There is **no** widely cited Unity production shader that clones Fortiche. The look is not a cel shader. It is hand-painted 3D, 2D FX, and per-shot compositing. For Unity, the transferable recipe is: painted diffuse as the source of truth, a hybrid unlit/PBR/rim shader, and 2D overlays.

---

## 1. Direct answer

Published studies examine how to recreate *Arcane* in real-time rendering.

What exists:

1. **First-party pipeline talks** (Fortiche CTO, matte-paint supervisor, SIGGRAPH / FMX / Foundry Mari) describing Maya + Photoshop/Mari + Nuke, proprietary shaders that sit painted textures on 3D, and treating every shot as a painting.
2. **Blender recreations** that actually look like the show: Noggi's $0 Jinx scene, Comfee procedural "Arcane" shaders, Cherylynn Lima (now at Riot) hand-paint tutorials, full environment paint-overs.
3. **Game-ready studies** that ask the Unity-relevant question: can this survive real-time? Best published answer is Arkady Nabirenkov's Toxic Cannon (Blender + Unreal): painted diffuse drives roughness and height; shader mixes unlit, PBR, rim, and a fake front light for the terminator.
4. **Unreal fan scenes and a Zaun FPS**, plus lighting mood studies. Almost no equivalent Unity writeup.

A toon ramp, Guilty Gear / Arc System Works shader, or fullscreen Kuwahara will not get you *Arcane*. Those are different families.

## 2. What the style actually is

Fortiche's own description: 3D characters and sets, **hand-painted textures**, **2D FX**, matte painting projected on simple geo, cinematic cameras, compositing that treats the frame as a painting. They call the blend the "Fortiche Touch" — drawing, 2D painting, and 3D in one image ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/animation/talent-experimentation-originality-how-fortiche-revolutionised-animated-storytelling-with-arcane/), [Yahoo / Fortiche S2](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/tv/articles/creative-team-behind-netflixs-arcane-090000492.html)).

RedShark's pipeline summary, matching Fortiche interviews:

- Stylized 3D with a **rough, tactile** finish, not smooth CG
- Hand-drawn FX (smoke, explosions, Jinx scribbles) often at **12 fps** over **24 fps** character animation
- Oil-paint environments; cel-like expression changes; vector/neon magic; charcoal / crayon / watercolor for psychological or musical beats (especially S2)
- Proprietary shaders to put painted textures on 3D
- Highlights and shadows **painted into textures**, not fully generated by CG lights
- Projection-mapped matte paintings for depth
- Keyframe only, no mocap
- Live-action camera language (handheld, zooms, limited "physical" cameras)

Sources: [RedShark](https://www.redsharknews.com/why-netflixs-arcane-looks-so-good-how-fortiche-ramped-up-the-animation-pipeline), [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/2022/06/arcane-netflix-league-of-legends-1234733820/), [SyncSketch / Alexis Wanneroy](https://blog.syncsketch.com/creator-stories/arcane-fortiche/), [80.lv backgrounds](https://80.lv/articles/arcane-artists-show-how-they-combine-traditional-art-3d-for-backgrounds).

Wanneroy: they wanted it **raw and graphic**, imperfections, **everything painted**, not traditional "appealing" CG character design. Piltover leans Art Nouveau / Art Deco clean; Zaun leans grimy European industrial Art Deco ([SyncSketch](https://blog.syncsketch.com/creator-stories/arcane-fortiche/), [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/riot-games-and-fortiche-get-revolutionary-with-arcane-season-2/)).

### Layer breakdown

| Layer | What Fortiche does | What they do not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Models | Appealing, slightly graphic 3D; hard-surface often sub-d with support loops | Photogrammetry, ultra-dense sculpts as the look |
| Albedo | Hand-painted oil-like strokes; bevels painted; some light/shadow baked in | Photo textures, heavy procedural fills |
| Lighting | Base / shadow / rim as a graphic structure; lots of painted light; compositors dose color per shot | Full PBR GI as the look; anime hard-cel as the look |
| FX | 2D drawings, 12 fps chaos vs 24 fps acting | Pure 3D volumes for every explosion |
| Sets | 3D blockout + matte paint projected; shaky freehand lines | Fully modeled hero geo for every background building |
| Post | Nuke as "make a painting"; film grain, lens dirt, scratches (esp. Jinx) | Screen-space oil filter as the style |

Season 2 adds charcoal funerals, watercolor memory, comic/music-video sequences, more Hexcore VFX ([AWN](https://www.awn.com/animationworld/riot-games-and-fortiche-push-every-possible-boundary-arcane-season-2), [FMX 2025](https://forticheprod.com/blog/projects-events/fortiche-rocks-fmx-2025-with-arcane-season-2-deep-dive/)).

### Software (official)

Maya 2018 (they stayed on it into S2), Photoshop, **Mari** for the painted look, Nuke, Houdini, After Effects. Rec. 709. Philippe Llerena (CTO): they do not chase new software; they go deep on known tools. Matte painting is 2D projected through the 3D camera, so cameras cannot roam freely or the projection breaks ([InCG / SIGGRAPH Asia 2024](https://www.incgmedia.com/makingof/siggraph-asia-2024-arcane-season-2), [Foundry: Arcane S2 texturing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCJIJG6Lz84)).

SIGGRAPH 2023 production session: *Fortiche: Crafting the Bridge* (Dupont, Terreaux, Llerena, Baudry) — pipeline and game-to-series design, not a published BRDF ([ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3577023.3585274)).

### Marketplace "Arcane shaders" (treat as NPR toys)

- CGTrader "Arcane style shader addon" for Blender: procedural, no-UV, mixed with Genshin. Not Fortiche's method.
- CG Lounge "NPR Painterly Shader Pack: Arcane and Anime" for Maya/Arnold: plug-and-play painterly, recommends hand-painted textures anyway.

- **Arktoon** / **Arc System Works** Unity shaders: Guilty Gear / FighterZ. Different problem (manual per-frame light, hard cel, ink outlines).

### Unity specifically

No GitHub or 80.lv hit for a production *Arcane* (Fortiche) Unity shader study comparable to Nabirenkov or Noggi.

What Unity *does* have, if you assemble a stack:

- URP/HDRP toon packs (Daniel Ilett Toon Shaders Pro, Silent's Cel Shading, etc.) for **ramps and rim** — useful as a lighting layer, not the look
- [tantaneity painterly URP post](https://jettelly.com/blog/recreating-a-painterly-shader-in-unity-urp/) — Voronoi brushstroke blit. Fine as a **background** softening pass (Comfee uses Kuwahara the same way). Do not put it on faces.
- Custom Shader Graph: unlit/Lit mix + rim + wrap, sampling a painted albedo

You will be porting the **Nabirenkov shader idea** into Shader Graph more than cloning a Unity repo.

## 5. What actually transfers to Unity

Fortiche lights and paints **per shot**. Unity lights **per frame, any camera**. You cannot ship their pipeline. You can ship their *material language* plus a shader that fakes the three-layer light.

### Do this (in order)

**1. Paint the diffuse. It is ~70% of the look.**

- Visible brush, structured not noisy. Paint bevels.
- Isolate surfaces, big strokes along form (Nabirenkov).
- Bake some AO / cavity / top-light into the albedo so it reads unlit (Fortiche; Noggi; Cherylynn).
- Tight value range on characters; let the grade add punch (Arcane Workflow video).
- Square / hard-round brushes show up constantly in recreations.

**2. Derive other maps from that diffuse.**

Nabirenkov's observation from frames: **roughness follows the painted diffuse**; **height follows the same strokes**. That is how metal "catches" only where the painter wanted, without a separately authored spec map.

**3. Hybrid shader, not a toon shader.**

Nabirenkov's real-time recipe, Unity-shaped:

| Control | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Unlit ↔ Lit (PBR) blend | Painted light stays; scene lights still move |
| Rim | Graphic edge light in almost every Fortiche shot |
| Fake front / wrap / terminator | Stops the rim from making an ugly 3D shadow line |
| AO intensity | Keeps cavities graphic |
| Specular from the derived roughness | Metal shimmer without raw GGX noise |

Optional: a second "shadow color" multiply (green in Zaun, warm in clubs) instead of black shadows.

**4. Faces**

Painted form shadows around nose, eyes, lips that **do not fully follow the key light**. Eye highlights often stay on even when the key is behind the head (noted in [Patreon lighting exploration](https://www.patreon.com/posts/arcane-lighting-69497266)). In Unity: painted face albedo + softer wrap than props; do not put Kuwahara on skin.

**5. 2D FX layer**

VFX Graph / flipbooks / mesh cards at a **lower effective frame rate** for explosions, smoke, hex, graffiti. This is as "Arcane" as the shader. Fortiche's 12-vs-24 split is cheap to fake and reads immediately.

**6. Sets**

Nightclub (Lucid Loop): simple geo + trim + **painted** materials; hero interaction surfaces denser; far walls can be a painted card or projected texture. Do not PBR every bottle.

**7. Grade**

LUT, bloom, a little grain/lens dirt. Per-scene palettes (Zaun green/chem, Piltover gold/teal, club neons). Comp is half the show.

### Do not do this

- Cel-shade a photoreal scene and call it Arcane
- Full-screen oil/Kuwahara on characters
- Guilty Gear ink outlines as the default
- Expect GI / Lumen / HDRP SSGI to "find" the look
- Procedural noise instead of strokes on hero assets

### Three-layer light in URP/HDRP terms

Noggi's reverse-eng, mapped to a game:

1. **Base** — painted albedo, slightly wrapped Lambert or unlit
2. **Shadow** — multiply a palette shadow color; optional extra mask (vertex paint or a lighting-ramp texture) for graphic shadow shapes
3. **Rim** — Fresnel or light-vs-view rim, colored, not white GGX

That is enough for a first Shader Graph.

### Lucid Loop notes

FigJam: Arcane-inspired character sheets, stylized 3D nightclub, ~45° high camera, close-up AI talk. That split matches Fortiche: **painted characters (tight values, graphic faces)** against **a more painterly set**, with FX and grade doing the rest. Club lighting (neons, rims, colored shadows) is closer to Zaun/Jinx palettes than to Piltover daylight.

HDRP gives SSS, volumes, and LUTs; URP is enough if the shader is custom and the textures are painted.
